What is the Founder’s Circle?
A limited-status layer for early bop Aero members designed to protect scheduling access, economics, flexibility, and insider participation as the platform grows.
Is the Founder’s Circle available to everyone?
No. The positioning is invitation-oriented and availability is intentionally limited.
What does “fee freeze protection” actually mean?
It refers to contractual protection against increases in defined annual ownership-related fees where applicable, subject to definitive program documents.
How does fleet exchange work?
Founder’s Circle members may receive cross-fleet access when capacity permits, with priority treatment relative to standard ownership access.
What happens if I decide to exit?
Founder’s Circle includes structured support around remarketing, transfer handling, and placement support where applicable.
